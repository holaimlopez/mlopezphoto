Marcelo L. Lopez Photography operates mlopezphoto.com. This policy describes information handled when you browse the portfolio or contact us.

## Information and use

When you email or text us, we receive the contact details and information you choose to share. We use these to answer inquiries and coordinate photography services. This site does not offer visitor accounts or an on-site contact form.

## Analytics and cookies

The site uses Google Analytics to measure visits and understand how people use the portfolio. Google may process device, browser, and interaction data and use cookies for analytics. See [Google’s explanation of data use on partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can manage cookies in your browser or use [Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## External services

Hosting, analytics, and externally loaded resources may process technical request data. Links to Instagram and other websites lead to services with their own privacy policies. Email and text communications also use third-party providers.

## Security and privacy requests

Electronic communications cannot be guaranteed secure. To ask about information you have provided, or request access, correction, or deletion, email [lopezlmarcelo@gmail.com](mailto:lopezlmarcelo@gmail.com). We will respond as applicable to your request and legal obligations.

## Updates and contact

Changes will appear here with a revised date. Contact Marcelo L. Lopez Photography in The Bronx, New York, at [lopezlmarcelo@gmail.com](mailto:lopezlmarcelo@gmail.com).
